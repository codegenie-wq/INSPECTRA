🚀 INSPECTION STUDIO

📋 INSPECTION CRITERIA

TRI-ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

INSPECTRA combines high-speed deep convolutional classification, unsupervised reconstruction anomaly detection, and visual gradient-weighted localization to audit hot-rolled and cold-rolled steel surfaces.

Every incoming frame or uploaded component undergoes synchronized physical product isolation, background suppression, defect classification across standardized classes, and open-set anomaly verification.

🧠 AI DEFECT CLASSIFICATION

MOBILENETV2 // 6 CLASSES

Fine-tuned deep convolutional classifier trained on NEU Surface Defect Database benchmark:

1. CRAZING
Micro-crack networks and stress fissures developing on the metal surface due to rapid thermal fluctuations, uneven rolling contraction, or surface embrittlement.
2. INCLUSION
Non-metallic impurities, slag entrapment, or refractory particles embedded into the molten steel during continuous casting and subsequent roll passes.
3. PATCHES
Irregular localized superficial oxide crusts, scabs, or lamination patches pressed flat into the metal substrate during roughing rolling stands.
4. PITTED SURFACE
Cavities, micro-voids, and localized chemical or acid-bath etch depressions caused by de-scaling inconsistencies or surface gas bubbles during cooling.
5. ROLLED-IN SCALE
Iron oxide mill scale fragments that failed hydraulic de-scaling and were subsequently rolled directly into the steel surface, causing dark indented streaks.
6. SCRATCHES
Linear abrasions, longitudinal gouges, and mechanical scoring inflicted by worn roll guides, transfer conveyors, or coiling friction along the rolling axis.

🔍 ANOMALY DETECTION

AUTOENCODER SCAN

The convolutional Autoencoder identifies visual deviations from learned pseudo-normal metal surface textures.

Unlike closed-set classifiers, the Autoencoder reconstructs the input image and computes the pixel-level Mean Squared Error (MSE). When the reconstruction residual surpasses the calibrated baseline, the component is flagged as an anomaly even if the defect pattern is novel or open-set.

🎯 DEFECT LOCALIZATION

GRAD-CAM HEATMAPS

Grad-CAM (Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping) calculates gradients flowing into the final convolutional feature maps with respect to the predicted defect category.

This provides direct visual evidence for the predicted defect region, projecting bounding coordinates and calculating the precise percentage of affected surface area.

IMPORTANT NOTICE: PROTOTYPE SEVERITY HEURISTICS

The thresholds below are PROTOTYPE SEVERITY HEURISTICS implemented for hackathon demonstration and experimental evaluation. They do NOT constitute official mill certifications or finalized ASTM industrial standards. Production deployment requires calibration against specific customer metallurgy tolerances.

LOW SEVERITY
< 1.0% AREA
Localized cosmetic surface imperfection with negligible impact on tensile or rolling integrity.
MEDIUM SEVERITY
1.0% – 5.0% AREA
Moderate surface anomaly requiring metallurgical review or secondary finishing polish before dispatch.
HIGH SEVERITY
> 5.0% AREA
Critical structural flaw exceeding acceptable ASTM tolerance. Automatic rejection and quarantine.
